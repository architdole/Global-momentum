        How this is built

        Prices: Yahoo Finance daily closes pulled by a GitHub Action (architdole/Global-momentum, market-feed/) three times a day; ETFs use adjusted close (total return), indices, FX, futures and crypto use price only. Factor ETFs: US iShares MSCI factor ETFs plus IWF, IWM, SPHB; developed ex-US iShares international factor ETFs; EM EEMO, FNDE, EEMV, EEMS; India NSE factor ETFs. News is researched and summarised by Claude on each scheduled run with a link to every source; it is not investment advice.
